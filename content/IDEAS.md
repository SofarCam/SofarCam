Type: Portrait / behind the scenes
Concept: The moment right before you call action. Show the prep — subject adjusting, Cam checking light, the quiet before the frame. No caption about magic. Talk about the trust required.

```
Nobody tells you how much of a shoot is just getting comfortable.

The light was set. The camera was ready. She wasn't — not yet.

So we talked for ten minutes about nothing. Her dog. Her commute. The coffee she didn't finish.

Then she was.

That's the shot.
```
