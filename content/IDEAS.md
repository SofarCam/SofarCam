### Shot by Seven

---

**SBS-001 | The shoot that almost didn't happen**
Type: Carousel or single image
Concept: Tell the story of a shoot where something went sideways — weather, location, client nerves, timing — and how it turned into one of the best frames of the session. Real, specific, no Hollywood ending needed.

Caption draft:
> We were 20 minutes from losing the light completely.
> Client was nervous. I was sweating.
> Changed locations on the fly, scrapped the original concept.
> This frame happened in the last five minutes.
> That's usually how it goes.
> The plan falls apart. The real shot shows up.
> #shotbyseven #charlottephotographer #portraitphotography #nodastudio

**SBS-002 | What I actually look at before I press the shutter**
Type: BTS photo or reel
Concept: Demystify the process. Where is the light coming from. What's in the background. Where the client's eyes are going. Short, punchy breakdown — not a tutorial, just a window into how you think on a shoot.

Caption draft:
> Before I shoot, I'm looking at three things.
> Light direction. What's behind you. Where your eyes want to go.
> Everything else is adjustable.
> The light is the only thing I can't fake.
> #behindthescenes #charlottephotographer #photographytips #shotbyseven

**SBS-003 | Charlotte light in May**
Type: Single image or carousel — natural light outdoor shoot
Concept: Lean into the season and the city. May light in Charlotte is specific — golden, humid, early evening hits different. Talk about shooting in NoDa or Plaza Midwood, what the city looks like through a lens right now.

Caption draft:
> May light in Charlotte doesn't last long.
> You get about 45 minutes before it's gone.
> Worth every early alarm to catch it.
> Shot in NoDa last week. Client showed up. Light showed up.
> That's all you need.
> #charlottephotographer #nodacharlotte #charlottenc #shotbyseven777
