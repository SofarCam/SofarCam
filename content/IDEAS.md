Platform: Instagram / Reels
Format: Talking head or carousel
Concept: Show a stunning shot taken on older/basic gear alongside a recent shot. The point: the difference between a good photographer and a bad one isn't the camera. Push back on the "what camera do you use" question directly.

Caption draft:
> People always ask what camera I shoot with.
>
> Here's the thing — I shot this on gear I bought three years ago. No new lens. No upgrade. Same camera as last year.
>
> The camera doesn't care how good the light is. You do.

> Stop waiting on gear. Learn your light. Learn your subject. Everything else is noise.
