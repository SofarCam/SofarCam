## Brand 1: Shot by Seven (@shotbyseven777)

**What it is:** Photography studio. Portraits, brand content, fashion, graduations, creative shoots.
**Site:** shotbyseven.com
**Handle:** @shotbyseven777

### Voice
Real, punchy, human. Short sentences. No fluff.
Talk about the person, the moment, the shoot.
NOT "Golden hour magic ✨" — that's not the vibe.

### What works
- The story behind a specific shoot
- The client's reaction, the moment before the shutter, the thing that almost went wrong
- Charlotte as context (NoDa, Plaza Midwood, Uptown, the light here in May)
- Process: what you looked for, what you changed on the fly
- Honest takes on the craft — gear, technique, client work vs. personal work

### What does NOT work
- Generic inspirational captions
- Emoji overload
- "Capturing memories that last a lifetime" type lines
- Passive voice

**What it is:** AI/tech content brand. Free tools + paid services for creators and businesses.
**Tools live at:** sofar.cam
**Services:** AI Agent Setup, AI Implementation Strategy, Custom AI Website

### Tools available (free)
- Concept Generator (3 viral ideas in 30s)
- Hook Writer (10 scroll-stopping hooks)
- Caption Writer (5 platform-ready captions)
- LinkedIn Writer (3 authority-building posts)
- Content Analyzer (break down any post)

### Paid services
- AI Agent Setup: $500–1,500 + $150–300/mo retainer
- AI Implementation Strategy: $500–2,000 (one-time)
- Custom AI Website: $1,500–5,000+

### Voice
Lead with the tool or insight, show the result, practical and direct.
No hype. No "AI will change everything." Show what it actually does.
Specific numbers, real outcomes, clear next step.

### What works
- "Here's what happened when I ran X through the tool"
- Side-by-side before/after on captions or hooks
- Specific use cases: "If you're a photographer on Instagram, here's your 3-second hook"
- Behind the build: what's being added, why
- The gap between expensive agency and generic ChatGPT
