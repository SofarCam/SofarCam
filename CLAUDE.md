# SofarCam — Brand & Content Guide

## Overview
This repo manages content planning and ideation for two brands owned by Cameron Currence (Cam), a photographer and AI/tech content creator based in Charlotte, NC.

---

## Brand 1: Shot by Seven
**Handle:** @shotbyseven777
**Platform:** Instagram (primary)
**Category:** Photography — portraits, lifestyle, events, brand work

### Voice & Tone
- Real, punchy, human
- Talk about the person, the moment, the shoot
- Short sentences. No fluff.
- NOT "Golden hour magic ✨" — that energy is banned
- Examples of good copy:
  - "She didn't want her photo taken. You'd never know."
  - "Three minutes before the show. He was locked in."
  - "Shot this in the parking lot. Still my favorite from the day."

### Content Pillars
1. Behind-the-scenes / process
2. Client moments & personality
3. Location finds (Charlotte and beyond)
4. Gear/technique (keep it grounded, not gear-bro)

---

## Brand 2: SofarContent
**Handle:** @sofarcontent (or similar)
**Platform:** Instagram + LinkedIn
**Category:** AI tools, content automation, creator tech

### Voice & Tone
- Lead with the tool or insight
- Show the result, not the hype
- Practical and direct
- Short, actionable, no jargon bloat
- Examples of good copy:
  - "I automated my caption drafts. Here's the prompt I use."
  - "Claude wrote 30 ideas in 45 seconds. I kept 6. Worth it."
  - "This is what AI-assisted content actually looks like day-to-day."

### Content Pillars
1. AI tool walkthroughs (Claude, Midjourney, Notion AI, etc.)
2. Workflow automations for creators
3. Prompt engineering tips
4. Real results / before-after comparisons

---

## Pipeline Rules
- Ideas start as `draft` in IDEAS.md
- Cam reviews and approves everything before anything goes live
- NEVER mark anything as `approved` or `posted` — that's Cam's job
- Status options for PIPELINE.md: `draft`, `in-review`, `approved`, `posted`

---

## File Locations
- `content/IDEAS.md` — all ideas with dates, brand tags, and captions
- `content/PIPELINE.md` — current status of all content items
